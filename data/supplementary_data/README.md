# Supplementary Data 1 to 7

Files cited as Supplementary Data in the manuscript. Column names match the derived tables in `../results/`.

**Supplementary Data 1** | Community water systems serving projected sites in all 20 runs, named, with population served, supply basis, average production, existing maximum day, mean-day and peak-day withdrawal demand of both designs, shares of the maximum day and of average production, and days per year above 25% and 100% of the maximum day (`Supplementary_Data_1a.csv`); and the monthly-reanalysis supply of every attributed system (`Supplementary_Data_1b.csv`).

**Supplementary Data 2** | Site-level results for all 16,645 site records of the 20 IM3 runs: location, climate cell, EIA-930 region and balancing authority, attributed water system at the 10,000-person and 3,300-person thresholds with distance, population and supply basis, estimated average production and existing maximum day, and the mean-day and peak-day withdrawal demand of the always-on evaporative and hybrid designs (`Supplementary_Data_2.csv`).

**Supplementary Data 3** | Storage-yield results by climate cell for the always-on evaporative design, the hybrid design and the always-on design under +1 and +2 C: mean and peak daily demand, peaking factors, water hours, storage for allocations of 1 to 2 times the mean day, dry days and electricity penalty of the capacity-storage grid and of the reserve rules, and municipal coincidence (`Supplementary_Data_3a.csv`); the storage curves (`Supplementary_Data_3b.csv`); and the constant-relative-humidity warming case (`Supplementary_Data_3c.csv`).

**Supplementary Data 4** | Capacity-aware siting results by state, scenario, headroom basis and share, demand regime, attribution threshold and design (`Supplementary_Data_4a.csv`), with the list of served, relocated and unserved sites (`Supplementary_Data_4b.csv`).

**Supplementary Data 5** | Cooling electricity and dry-backup increments at the peak hours of each EIA-930 region, each state and the contiguous United States for every scenario (`Supplementary_Data_5a.csv`), and the hourly national profiles of the grid peak day (`Supplementary_Data_5b.json`).

**Supplementary Data 6** | Least-cost results: annualized cost of every capacity-storage combination by cell (`Supplementary_Data_6a.csv`), the least-cost choice of each cell with the break-even capital cost of dry backup (`Supplementary_Data_6b.csv`), the one-at-a-time sensitivity (`Supplementary_Data_6c.csv`) and the Monte Carlo draws (`Supplementary_Data_6d.csv`).

**Supplementary Data 7** | Validation: results at the 46 NOAA ISD stations and their POWER cells (`Supplementary_Data_7a.csv`), the summary statistics (`Supplementary_Data_7b.csv`) and the five measured-facility cases (`Supplementary_Data_7c.csv`).
